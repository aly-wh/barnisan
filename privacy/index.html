Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version: our apps do not collect your data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers to send anything to.

Who this covers

This policy is published by Barnisan LLC ("we", "us"), an independent software studio in the United States. It applies to the following app:

  • YapOff — a pass-and-play party word game for iPhone and iPad.

If we publish further apps, this policy will be updated to list them explicitly.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. YapOff has no network functionality whatsoever — it cannot send information anywhere, because it never makes a network request.

Specifically, the app contains:

  • No user accounts or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track
  • No third-party SDKs or frameworks

Player names

Player names typed in during setup exist only in the device's memory for the duration of a game. They are never written to storage, never leave the device, and are gone when you close the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, or files.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, of any age.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer information to third parties, because we do not hold any information to share. Note that if you download an app from the App Store, your relationship with Apple is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Changes to this policy

If our apps ever begin collecting data, we will update this page before that change ships, and revise the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via barnisan.com/#contact.
