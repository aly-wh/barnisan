About

Barnisan LLC is an independent software company based in the United States. We're a small studio by design: one product at a time, built with care, and maintained for the long run.

Our current focus is consumer apps for Apple platforms, alongside software for live, interactive communities — a space we know well from years on the creator side of streaming.

What we do

Community & live tools
