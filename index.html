About

Barnisan is an independent software company based in the United States. We're a small studio by design: a handful of titles in development at once, each built with care and maintained for the long run.

We make games for Apple platforms. We come at it from years on the creator side of streaming, which is where the interest in things people play together came from.

What we're making

Everything is designed and developed in-house, from first sketch to release.
