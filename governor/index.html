Governor: Free Republic

Sink pirates. Found colonies.

One frigate, a charter, and a sea full of pirates. Found settlements, raise a fleet, sink the raiders and bank their plunder — for as long as your term holds.

In development

For iPhone and iPad. Email us if you’d like to hear when there’s news.

How it plays

You are the Governor. The Republic handed you a charter, one frigate, and a stretch of open water.

Take the sea

Drag a course, lock on a target

Tap a ship and drag its course. Tap a pirate to lock on. Idle ships engage anything that comes near, so a patrol left in the right place is worth a hundred taps later.

Hold the coast

Settle islands that suit the build

Colony ships carry settlers to empty islands. Every island has a character — fertile, forested, pastoral, quarry — and takes only the buildings that suit it.

What is out there

Thirteen classes, escalating

Pirates come wave by wave. Five named flagships sail under captains out of the history books, each carrying a bounty worth the risk — and in deep water, a kraken that hunts pirate and Republic alike.

Letters of Marque

Draft your term before you sail

Boons cost points and burdens pay for them. Take Veteran Crews and Long Guns, and answer for it with Crown Tariffs and Wanted Posters. Every run is a different bargain.

Drawn in code

No art files. No sound files. The game draws and plays itself.

Every ship, island, building and icon is pixel art generated on the device at runtime — down to the app icon. So is the audio: the cannon fire, the bosun’s whistle, the sea and the shanty are synthesised as the game runs, with no sound files shipped at all.
