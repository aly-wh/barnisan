A party word game about building gloriously terrible sentences out of whatever words you happen to be dealt. 2–8 players, one device, no internet.

For iPhone and iPad. Email us if you’d like to hear when there’s news.

How it plays

Someone has to explain why the goose was at the wedding. Tonight, that someone is you.

A prompt goes up

One prompt on screen for everyone to answer — somewhere between mildly unhinged and fully feral.

You get 20 random words

Dealt in balanced hands, so there's always something to build with. Thirty seconds on the clock.

Pass the phone along

No peeking. Everyone taps together the funniest answer they can manage out of the hand they were given.

The judge picks a winner

All the answers get read out. First player to 5 prompt cards takes the game.

The prompt

Write a review of a gym run by monks.

Your words — tap to add

huh terrible orchestrated across immediately toilet officiated fragile client-ready borrowed religious politician stars

You never get the words you want. You get alpaca, respectfully and plunger, and you have to make it work anyway. The terrible hands are the best ones.

Privacy

It doesn't collect anything, because there's nowhere for it to send anything.

No accounts No internet No ads No tracking No data collected

YapOff has no networking code in it at all — no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. Player names live in memory for the length of a game and are gone when you close it. Read the full privacy policy.
